# Physics Informed Neural Networks for ChemoMechanics Problems
###The above workbook is made to train and answer two subproblems in ChemoMechanics from open datasets:
## Hydrogen Embrittlement in Zirconium Alloys
Within nuclear reactors, Zirconium alloys undergo metallurgical degredation where the Zirconium becomes brittle and prone to cracking. Hydrogen released from initial oxidation reactions migrate along stress and thermal gradients within the Zr alloy.

## Dendrite Growth on LLZO
For the solid state electrolyte material Li$_{7}$La$_{3}$Zr$_{2}$O$_{12}$ LLZO, Li dendrite growth from the anode penetrates LLZO, leaving a route for active material loss on the anode, degredation of the solid elecrolyte material, and eventual contact with the cathode material.
